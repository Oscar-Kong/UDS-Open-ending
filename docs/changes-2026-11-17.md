# Changes, 17 November 2026

This note covers the work since the chat was first pushed. The survey item is unchanged: whether Northeastern students feel they have an advantage over peers who did not attend, and why.

## How an answer is built

The hard filter still runs first. Four counted passes then sit above the paragraph: scope, stance, reasons, and whether the slice is enough. The model writes one paragraph from those figures only. Quotations stay beside the paragraph. If the model call fails, the paragraph is the filtered headline and the accuracy panel says so.

A count or a reason ranking draws a bar chart. A comparison draws a table. A question the comments cannot answer, or a slice that is too small, stays as text with no chart and no reason ranking.

Each answer ends with an accuracy check: every number in the paragraph is in the filtered counts, each quotation matches an explanation, and a thin slice is refused. The same checks run with `python3 pipeline/check_accuracy.py`.

## The model

The settings are a general LLM key, endpoint, and model name. The endpoint is Dash at `https://api-llm.dashlab.studio/v1`. That host does not serve `gemma4:latest`. The app uses `gemma4:31b`. `nemotron3:33b` and `qwen3.8:27b` are also listed by `GET /models` and can be typed into Library. The key stays in `.env`, which is not committed.

## Opening screen

The first screen lists nine sample questions in three groups: Bar chart, Table, and No chart. On a narrow window the groups stack. New chat brings the list back.

## Ten questions for a demo

1. What is the main reason students feel they have an advantage?
2. How many say yes, and how many say no?
3. Where do students say there is no real advantage?
4. What do Khoury students in 2024 say about co-op?
5. What do Bouve students say about co-op?
6. How do undergraduate and graduate students differ?
7. What changed in the reasons after 2020?
8. How do engineering and business school students differ?
9. What is the average starting salary?
10. What do law students in 2016 say about their advantage?

The first five draw a bar chart. The next three draw a comparison table. The last two do not draw a chart.
